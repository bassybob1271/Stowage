Questions

What happens if I downgrade in the middle of a month?

The change happens immediately. The days you already paid for and didn't use go back to your wallet as credits, so you can come back to Flex later without paying twice.

What if I'm over the free space when I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. Uploads pause until you're back under the free space or back on Flex. You can still open, play and download everything.

How do credits work?

Buy a pack by card and the credits land in your wallet. Flex renews from the wallet every 30 days, and extra storage and paid sends come out of it too. Bigger packs include bonus credits.

Can I get my money back?

Unused purchased credits are refundable when you close your account. Contact support and we'll process it.

Where are my files stored?

On Backblaze B2, always-hot cloud storage with data centers in North America and Europe. Your files sit in a private space only your account can open.

Is BazzyBox part of Bazzy TV?

Yes. It's the storage side of Bazzy TV, made for the same filmmakers and musicians who stream and publish there.
